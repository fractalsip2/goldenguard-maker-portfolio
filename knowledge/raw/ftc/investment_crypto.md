# Investment and Cryptocurrency Scams

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/investment-scams
Category: investment_crypto
Description: Guidance on guaranteed returns, fake trading platforms, and cryptocurrency investment pitches.

Investment scammers may promise large or guaranteed profits, claim there is little or no risk, and direct victims to fake websites or apps that display invented account growth. They often pressure people to deposit more money or cryptocurrency.

Do not invest based on an unsolicited message. Independently research the company and remember that legitimate investments cannot guarantee profits.
