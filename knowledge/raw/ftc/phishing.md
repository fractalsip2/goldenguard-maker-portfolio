# How To Recognize and Avoid Phishing Scams

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-avoid-phishing-scams
Category: unknown
Description: General guidance for recognizing suspicious texts and emails that seek passwords, account details, or money.

Phishing messages often impersonate banks, government agencies, delivery services, or familiar companies. Common warning signs include unexpected links, urgent threats, requests for passwords or verification codes, and website addresses that do not match the real organization.

Do not click an unexpected link. Contact the organization through its official website, app, statement, or a trusted phone number instead.
