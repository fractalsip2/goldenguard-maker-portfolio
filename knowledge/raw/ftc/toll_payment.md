# Got a Text About Unpaid Tolls? It’s Probably a Scam

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/01/got-text-about-unpaid-tolls-its-probably-scam
Category: toll_payment
Description: Official guidance for checking toll claims without using links in unexpected texts.

Scammers send texts claiming that an unpaid toll must be paid immediately. The link may lead to a phishing page that requests payment-card or identity information.

Do not click or reply. Contact the state tolling agency using a website or phone number you already know is real, then report and delete the message.
