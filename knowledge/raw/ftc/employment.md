# Job Scams

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams
Category: employment
Description: Official guidance on fake jobs, fake checks, equipment purchases, and employer impersonation.

Job scammers may offer work without a normal interview, ask for financial or identity information early, or send a check for equipment and instruct the applicant to send money back. The check may appear in an account before the bank later discovers that it is fake.

Do not deposit a check from an unverified employer or pay for promised work. Confirm the position through the employer’s official website and contact the company directly.
