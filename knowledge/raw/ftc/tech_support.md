# How To Spot, Avoid, and Report Tech Support Scams

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-spot-avoid-and-report-tech-support-scams
Category: tech_support
Description: Guidance on fake virus warnings, remote-access requests, and fraudulent support payments.

Tech-support scammers may display a frightening pop-up or call unexpectedly and claim that a computer is infected. They often request remote access, payment, passwords, or financial information.

Do not call a number shown in an unexpected pop-up, install remote-access software, or allow a stranger to control the device. Contact the company through its official website.
