# Avoiding and Reporting Gift Card Scams

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/avoiding-and-reporting-gift-card-scams
Category: gift_card
Description: Guidance on payment demands involving gift cards and what to do after sharing a card number or PIN.

Scammers may pretend to be a business, government agency, employer, friend, or relative and insist that a problem can only be solved with gift cards. Requests for the card number, PIN, or a photo of the back of the card are strong warning signs.

Do not purchase or send gift cards as payment. If a card was already shared, contact the gift-card company promptly, keep the card and receipt, and report the fraud.
