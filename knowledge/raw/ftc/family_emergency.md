# Scammers Use Fake Emergencies To Steal Your Money

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/scammers-use-fake-emergencies-steal-your-money
Category: grandparent_emergency
Description: Guidance on calls and messages that impersonate relatives and demand urgent, secret payments.

Family-emergency scammers may claim that a child or grandchild was arrested, injured, or stranded. They often demand immediate payment and tell the recipient not to contact anyone else.

Pause before sending money. Call the relative or another family member using a number you already know. A secret payment request is a major warning sign.
