# Selling Stuff Online? Here’s How to Avoid a Scam

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2022/07/selling-stuff-online-heres-how-avoid-scam
Category: marketplace
Description: Official guidance on fake payments, overpayments, checks, and unsafe online sales.

A fake buyer may send a check for more than the sale price, claim the extra amount was accidental, and ask the seller to return the difference. The bank may initially make the funds available even though the check is fake.

Do not accept a check for more than the selling price, do not return an overpayment, and verify payments inside the marketplace or payment application before releasing an item.
