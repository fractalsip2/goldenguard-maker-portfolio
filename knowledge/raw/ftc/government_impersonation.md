# How To Avoid a Government Impersonation Scam

Source: FTC
Source URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-government-impersonation-scam
Category: government_impersonation
Description: Guidance on fake agency calls, texts, threats, and immediate payment demands.

Government impersonators may claim to represent the IRS, Social Security Administration, Medicare, law enforcement, or another agency. They may threaten arrest, benefit suspension, or legal action and demand payment or personal information.

Do not pay or provide information in response to an unexpected contact. Look up the agency's official .gov website and contact it through a verified channel.
