# How Fake Employment Scams Target Your Online Job Search

Source: AARP
Source URL: https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/online-job-search/
Category: employment
Description: Guidance on fake recruiters, remote-job offers, and requests for money or personal information.

Fake recruiters may contact job seekers by text or email, impersonate a real company, conduct interviews only through messaging, and offer a position unusually quickly. Some send a fake check and instruct the applicant to buy equipment or send part of the money elsewhere.

Do not pay to get a job, deposit an unexpected check, or buy equipment from a required vendor. Verify the opening and recruiter through the company’s official website and independently obtained contact information.
