# How to Protect Yourself Against Online Romance Scams

Source: AARP
Source URL: https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/romance/
Category: romance
Description: Guidance for recognizing online relationships that gradually lead to requests for money or investments.

Romance scammers build trust through frequent messages and emotional attention, but avoid meeting in person. Eventually, they may request money for travel, medical problems, an emergency, or a supposed investment.

Do not send money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or account information to an online love interest you have not verified. Discuss the relationship with someone you trust.
