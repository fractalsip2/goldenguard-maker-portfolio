# How to Protect Yourself from Bank Impersonation Scams

Source: AARP
Source URL: https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/bank-impersonation-fake-text-messages-emails-calls/
Category: bank_impersonation
Description: Guidance for recognizing fake bank texts, calls, and emails and verifying account problems safely.

Bank impersonation messages often claim that an account is locked, a payment is suspicious, or immediate verification is required. They may use a bank's name while directing the recipient to a lookalike website or asking for a password or verification code.

Do not use the link or phone number in an unexpected message. Open the bank's official app, type its website address yourself, or call the number printed on the back of the card.
