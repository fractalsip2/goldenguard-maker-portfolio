# How to Identify and Avoid Facebook Marketplace Scams

Source: AARP
Source URL: https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/facebook-marketplace/
Category: marketplace
Description: Guidance on fake buyers, fake payments, verification-code requests, and marketplace fraud.

Marketplace scammers may use newly created accounts, send fake payment notices, claim they overpaid, ask for a refund, or request a verification code to prove that a seller is real.

Keep communication and payment on the marketplace when possible. Do not refund an alleged overpayment until the payment is independently verified, and never share a verification code.
