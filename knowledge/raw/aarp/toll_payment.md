# How to Protect Yourself Against Toll Road Scam Texts

Source: AARP
Source URL: https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/toll-road-texts.html
Category: toll_payment
Description: Guidance on texts that impersonate toll-collection systems and demand immediate payment.

Toll-road scam texts claim that a small unpaid balance will lead to penalties or legal consequences. The link may lead to a lookalike site that collects card, driver’s-license, or other personal information.

Do not use the link in an unexpected toll text. Find the tolling agency’s official website yourself and check any balance through a verified channel.
