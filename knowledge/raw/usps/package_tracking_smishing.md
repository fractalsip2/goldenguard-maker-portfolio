# USPS Package Tracking Text Scams

Source: U.S. Postal Inspection Service
Source URL: https://www.uspis.gov/news/scam-article/smishing-package-tracking-text-scams
Category: package_delivery
Description: Official guidance on unexpected delivery texts, unfamiliar links, and reporting USPS-related smishing.

Scammers send unexpected messages claiming that a package cannot be delivered, an address must be updated, or a small fee must be paid. These messages often contain an unfamiliar tracking link and request payment or personal information.

Do not click the link. Visit USPS.com directly or use the official USPS app. USPS-related smishing can be reported to spam@uspis.gov and forwarded to 7726.
